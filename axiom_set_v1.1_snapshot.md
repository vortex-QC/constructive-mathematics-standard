## What changed in v1.1

Geometry-layer additions only; the v1.0 axiom text is kept **verbatim** (axiom minimalism maintained; same precedent as the boundary-four-force G-Ⅲ corollary entry):

1. **G-Ⅱ corollary entry — "the irreversibility of abstract projection"**: the mapping from energy form to geometric form is a one-way projection; the instantaneous shape structure (sign / support / amplitude distribution) survives, the time-derivative structure (growth rate / frequency / timing) is dropped; inversion is many-to-one, exact recovery is impossible; the collapse rate worsens monotonically as the injection space grows. Evidence chain: five pre-registered experiment batches (PR6–PR10), closed by PR11's negative result on the axis-family extension (2026-09-21).
2. **G-Ⅰ reverse-reading note** (note form, not entered into the axiom text): the reverse reading of G-Ⅰ is constrained by the G-Ⅱ corollary — only the shape-structure survival set is partially recoverable.

Observational origin of the corollary: the maintainer's question of 2026-09-18 ("why can the energy form not be recovered from geometry — because the boundless-motion axiom makes geometry an abstract form"), anchored verbatim in the internal provenance file.
